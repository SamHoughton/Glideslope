Install the firmware on a Huidu HD-WF2 from your browser. Version ….

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial is needed; Safari and Firefox don't have it).
  2. First install only: put the board in download mode. With the 5 V supply unplugged and USB connected, bridge the round (GND) and square (GPIO0) holes on the left edge of the module, then plug in the 5 V. The board's USB port does not power it.
  3. Press Install, pick the board's serial port, and wait about a minute.
  4. Unplug the 5 V and plug it back in. Join the Glideslope-Setup Wi-Fi network and enter your home Wi-Fi. The panel then shows its address: http://glideslope.local/.
This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer needs a secure (https) page.

Already running Glideslope? Update from the board's own web page instead, with firmware.bin from the latest release; your settings are kept. Airline logos are not included (see the README).
