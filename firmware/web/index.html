    Where to look

    Uses the home marker (Airport tab). An aircraft passing within the distance below crosses the panel at a size set by its height, with where it is going. Each aircraft once every 45 minutes.

    A watchlist aircraft anywhere in range takes over the panel and, with alerts on, sends one to your phone (once every 6 hours each).

    Alerts on your phone through ntfy: free, no account. Install the ntfy app and subscribe to the topic below. Anyone who knows a topic can read it, so keep it long and random.

    Home Assistant

    Sensors over MQTT with discovery: next arrival, runways in use, today's arrivals and departures, aircraft holding, the last flight. Blank host = off.

    The board's logbook: a record of every day, every go-around and rare sighting, and every airline and aircraft type it has ever seen. It starts with the first midnight after the update.
